# NeoVerse Product Launch Video Brief

## Workflow

- **Workflow:** product-launch-video
- **Deliverable:** A premium launch film for the NeoVerse inspection-first commerce experience.
- **Target duration:** 45–60 seconds
- **Primary format:** 16:9 landscape, with framing that can be adapted to 9:16 later
- **Audience:** Shoppers researching higher-consideration physical products; technology enthusiasts; merchants and product teams interested in inspection-led commerce.
- **Source material:** NeoVerse storefront at `https://neoverse.sujit.top/`, repository UI and product media, and documented product capabilities only.

## Product truth

NeoVerse helps people reduce uncertainty before checkout. It brings product imagery, dimensions, materials, availability, price, comparison, 3D inspection, and supported AR/spatial inspection into one decision path.

The film must not imply that every product supports every immersive feature. Show capability labels and device support only when the product data supports them.

## Core message

> Inspect the object. Understand the fit. Decide with confidence.

## Story premise

A normal product image leaves questions unanswered. NeoVerse turns those questions into evidence: what it is made of, how large it is, whether it is available, what it costs, and how it looks in your space. The launch film moves from uncertainty to a calm, informed purchase decision.

## Creative direction

### Visual thesis

**Instrumented atelier:** a quiet, tactile product atelier with precise measurement and commerce evidence built into the experience.

### Tone

- Tactile
- Editorial
- Precise
- Calmly confident
- Premium, but not glossy
- Product-led rather than effect-led

### Palette

Use the existing NeoVerse design system:

- Deep graphite / ink: `#080B12`
- Raised ink: `#0D121A`
- Panel: `#121922`
- Warm paper: `#F1EFE8`
- Muted blue-gray: `#A3ACB8`
- Electric interaction blue: `#89A6FF`
- Sparse lime confirmation: `#C7ED70`

Avoid purple-blue gradients, neon cyberpunk treatments, fake holograms, decorative particles, and glassmorphism.

### Typography

- Display: Space Grotesk
- Interface/body: Inter
- Sentence case, left-aligned where possible
- Sparse copy; no fake metrics or invented testimonials

## Narrative structure

1. **Problem:** A flat image cannot answer every buying question.
2. **Reframe:** NeoVerse makes inspection part of commerce.
3. **Evidence:** Product details, dimensions, material, stock, price, comparison.
4. **Inspection:** 3D and supported AR/spatial views help the shopper understand scale and fit.
5. **Decision:** A clear, server-validated purchase path reduces surprises.
6. **Close:** NeoVerse and the core promise.

## Voiceover draft

"A product photo can show you what something looks like. But not how it fits, what it is made of, or whether it is the right choice. NeoVerse brings the evidence closer. See the details. Check the dimensions. Compare what matters. Inspect supported products in 3D or in your space. Then move from uncertainty to a decision you can trust. NeoVerse — inspect the object. Decide with confidence."

The narration may be shortened during edit to preserve breathing room and let the product interface carry the story.

## Sound direction

- Quiet, tactile electronic bed; restrained pulse rather than cinematic trailer music.
- Subtle interface clicks and low material sounds for evidence reveals.
- No aggressive risers, sci-fi impacts, or overprocessed voice.
- Final sonic moment should be a clean, short confirmation tone.

## Must show

- A real NeoVerse product image or product stage.
- Product name, dimensions/material evidence, price, and availability as visible facts.
- Catalog/search or comparison behavior.
- 3D inspection or a clearly supported immersive inspection state.
- A purchase/checkout decision surface without suggesting checkout is universally production-complete.
- NeoVerse wordmark/logo mark at the end.

## Must not show

- Fake user counts, conversion rates, testimonials, or awards.
- Unsupported AR claims or universal device compatibility.
- Generic AI assistant chat as the hero feature.
- Decorative HUDs, floating labels, particles, or random holograms.
- A claim that MongoDB/API/catalog synchronization is fully complete.

## End card

**NeoVerse**

Inspect the object. Understand the fit. Decide with confidence.

`neoverse.sujit.top`

## Production notes

- Prefer real product UI capture and repository assets over generated UI.
- Preserve the product’s asymmetric editorial layout and evidence-first hierarchy.
- Motion should explain an action: open, inspect, compare, place, confirm.
- Keep the object visually legible at every beat.
- Design for reduced-motion-safe still frames as well as animated output.
