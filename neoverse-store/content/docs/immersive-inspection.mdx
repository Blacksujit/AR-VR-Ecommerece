# Immersive inspection

3D, AR, and VR are decision tools, not decoration. They are useful when a customer needs to understand scale, placement, material, or spatial fit before buying.

## Capability states

Every immersive surface should make its state explicit:

- **Available:** the device and product provide a supported experience.
- **Unsupported:** the current device or browser cannot provide it.
- **Unavailable:** the product does not have the required model or asset.
- **Loading:** the experience is preparing; preserve the stage dimensions.
- **Failed:** explain what failed and offer image inspection as a fallback.

## Product stage rules

1. Preserve a stable media frame to avoid layout shift.
2. Keep object controls close to the object, not buried in marketing copy.
3. Make image inspection the reliable fallback.
4. Do not imply AR support when the product data does not confirm it.
5. Load heavy immersive dependencies behind user intent when possible.
6. Disable nonessential motion for reduced-motion users.

## Why this matters

A failed model should not become a failed product page. The customer must still be able to understand the object, see its evidence, and make a safe next decision.
