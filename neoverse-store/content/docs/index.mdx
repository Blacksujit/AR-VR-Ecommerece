# See it before it arrives

NeoVerse helps people make better decisions about physical products online. Instead of asking shoppers to trust a polished image, it brings useful evidence closer to the moment of choice: dimensions, materials, availability, price, and spatial inspection where the device supports it.

<Callout type="info" title="The short version">
NeoVerse is an instrumented product showroom, not a generic marketplace and not an AI demo.
</Callout>

## What NeoVerse is solving

Online product pages often answer “does this look good?” while leaving harder questions unresolved:

- Will it fit the room, desk, or body I have in mind?
- What are the useful dimensions and materials?
- Is the displayed price and stock still trustworthy?
- Can I compare it with a credible alternative?

The product, catalog, and checkout experience are designed around reducing those uncertainties before payment.

## Product surface map

| Surface | What it helps the customer do |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Understand why inspection improves a purchase decision |
| Catalog | Compare real objects quickly |
| Product detail | Resolve uncertainty and choose the next action |
| Showroom | Inspect an object spatially without turning shopping into a game |
| Cart and checkout | Verify selections, cost, and payment state |
| Dashboard | Track saved objects, orders, and account information |
